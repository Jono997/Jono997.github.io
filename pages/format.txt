subpages (pages stored in this directory) are formatted into sections, each section starting with a heading.
The heading is formatted as such: "[section_name]". A heading must be at the start of a line with no indenting.
All lines after a heading are counted as being part of that section until the next heading or the end of the file is reached.
Sections will be parsed and inserted into the page differently depending on their section name.

head: Contents will be inserted to the start of the page's head element.
main: Contents will replace the contents of the page's main element's innerHTML.
body: Contents will be inserted to the start of the page's body element.
body-end: Contents will be inserted to the end of the page's body element.
